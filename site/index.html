How to look around

  • ↔ Use the arrows at the left and right to turn. Hold one down to keep turning.
  • ✋ Or hold the mouse button and drag to look around.
  • ⭕ Click anywhere on the floor to walk to that part of the room.
  • 🏠 Use the buttons at the bottom to jump straight to a room.
  • ▶ Press “Show me around” and it will walk you through by itself.
